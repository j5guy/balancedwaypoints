Balanced Waypoints is a self-hosted, single-household budgeting app: accounts,
transactions, payees, categories, tags, envelope budgeting, rules, and
schedules, in the same Node.js/Express/MongoDB shape as its sibling projects
`workouts` and `fondwaypoints`.
